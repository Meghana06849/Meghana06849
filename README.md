  - Built with React, Tailwind, Node.js, MongoDB, Socket.IO.  
  - Why it mattered: designed defaults to reduce notification anxiety and keep conversations readable.

- Mood Matcher — a small utility to surface emotional patterns and offer tiny, research-backed suggestions.

  - Why it mattered: low-friction tracking + actionable nudges increased engagement in prototypes.
